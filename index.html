About me

I’m a graphic designer with a strong background in marketing. I focus on building consistent brands across every touchpoint, making sure everything connects and makes sense. I believe brand strategy is the foundation of any successful project, and marketing is an essential part of making that strategy work in real life. When both are aligned, everything else follows. Adaptability is one of my strengths. I’m comfortable working across different areas, learning fast, and stepping into new challenges without overthinking it.
